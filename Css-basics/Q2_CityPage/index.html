Kyoto, Japan

Overview Culture Travel Tips

A City of Timeless Beauty

Kyoto blends ancient temples with modern life in a way few cities can.

Overview

Once the imperial capital of Japan, Kyoto is home to over 1,600 temples, 400 shrines, and beautifully preserved traditional wooden houses.

Culture

From tea ceremonies to geisha districts, Kyoto keeps centuries-old traditions alive alongside its modern neighborhoods.

Travel Tips

Visit in spring for cherry blossoms or autumn for fiery maple leaves. Rent a bicycle to explore the old town at your own pace.
